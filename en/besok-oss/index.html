Visit us

The farm shop is open around the clock

You are welcome at Bergsmyrene. The farm shop is self-service and open all year. Through the summer the village store, the bakery and the pick-your-own flower garden are open too.

Pick your own

Through the summer the flower garden is open for picking. Take what you want and pay in the farm shop, like everything else here.

Loose weight, less packaging

We avoid plastic packaging wherever we can, and in the farm shop you can choose loose weight. Bring a bag or a box.
