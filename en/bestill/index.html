Order

Order during the week, collect on Thursday

Order in the online shop, we harvest and pack, and your bag is waiting at a pickup point near you on Thursday. Collection is free. Home delivery is available in some periods.

How it works

Four steps

  1. 01

    Order

    Order in the online shop and pay with Vipps, card or Klarna. You get a confirmation by email.

  2. 02

    Deadline

    The order deadline is Monday at 23:59. Orders placed after that are delivered the following Thursday.

  3. 03

    We harvest

    We harvest on tuesday and wednesday, so that everything is ready to pack for Thursday's run.

  4. 04

    Collect

    Your bag goes to the pickup point you chose, in Hurum, Oslo or Asker. Collect it within that place's opening hours.

Packaging

Bring the box back

We deliver in boxes. Please bring the box back on your next delivery, or bring a bag of your own to carry the goods home in.

We aim to use as little packaging as possible, and avoid plastic in particular. But to keep the produce fresh we do use packaging where it is needed.
