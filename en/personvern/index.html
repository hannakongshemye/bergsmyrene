Privacy

Privacy policy

What we collect on bergsmyrene.no, why, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Last updated: 2026-10-07

Who is responsible

Severin Romer Iversen (org. no. 930694231), Bergsmyrene 2, 3483 Kana, is the data controller for personal data processed through this website. You can reach us at kontakt@bergsmyrene.no or +47 400 73 434.

What this site collects

This website is a static site. It has no user accounts, no comment field and no tracking of individuals.

The site currently sets no cookies at all, and no visit measurement is running. If we switch statistics on later, nothing will be loaded before you have given consent, and you will be asked first.

If you write to us through the contact form or by email, we keep what you write for as long as it takes to answer you and to handle any order it relates to.

If you sign up for the newsletter, we store your email address until you unsubscribe. Every newsletter carries an unsubscribe link.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies. There is no tracking, no advertising and no visit measurement.

The online shop

Orders and payment are handled in the online shop, which runs on a separate platform with its own privacy policy and its own terms. When you follow the link to the shop, you leave this website.

Who else sees the data

The site is hosted by Vercel, which processes technical log data as part of serving the pages. Email reaches us through our ordinary email provider.

We never sell personal data, and we never pass it on except where it is needed to fulfil an agreement with you or where the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and withdraw consent at any time. Write to us and we will deal with it.

If you think we are handling your data wrongly, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.
